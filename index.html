About Our NGO

Building a better future together

InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a youth-led non-profit organization focused on creating positive social change through impactful campaigns, education programs, awareness drives, and humanitarian support.


The organization believes that small actions combined together can transform communities and inspire long-term social impact.

Our Projects

Initiatives that are changing lives

Project BachpanShala

Supporting underprivileged children with quality education, mentorship, workshops, and digital learning opportunities.

Project Udaan

Empowering women through entrepreneurship training, leadership workshops, and self-development initiatives.

Project Prakriti

Promoting environmental sustainability through plantation drives, awareness campaigns, and eco-friendly initiatives.

Animal Welfare

Caring for stray animals through feeding drives, rescue support, and awareness about compassion and care.
